Material first, always.

Every project starts with a decision: grain, gloss, and grade. We help you choose the laminate or veneer that fits how the room is actually used — then cut, edge-band, and assemble it ourselves, in our own workshop, before fitting it on site.

No subcontracted guesswork. The people who measure your room are the same people who install the finished piece.

How a project runs, start to finish

01

Consult

We visit the space, take real measurements, and talk through how each room gets used.

02

Select material

Choose laminate, veneer, and finish from physical swatches — not a screen.

03

Fabricate

Panels are cut, edge-banded, and assembled in our own workshop.

04

Install & finish

Fitted on site, levelled, hardware set, and cleaned up before we leave.
